## Tips for new weeks

- One clear action per picture; the target object must be visible (book, toothbrush, ball…).
- Do not ask for words, signs or labels in the picture. Put all English in the JSON files.
- For habits (every day / never), use the calendar strip in the data, not the picture.
- Avoid gender stereotypes: both Mia and Sam do sports, reading, cooking, etc.
- After generating, look at the full-size image: hands, objects, faces, extra fingers, hidden text.
